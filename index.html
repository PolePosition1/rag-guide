The Mechanic

So what is RAG?

A raw AI model only knows what it was trained on — frozen at a cutoff date, no live web. RAG bolts a search step onto the front of it. Before answering, the system retrieves relevant, current pages from Google's index, then generates a response grounded in what those pages actually say. Retrieve, then generate. That's the whole name — and it's why AI search can hand you a fresh, sourced answer instead of a confident guess.

The Playbook

Three rules Google told us to follow

Not hacks. Not "AEO secrets." These come straight out of Google's own documentation — and Google spends a whole section debunking the gimmicks people are selling instead.

01

Write non-commodity content

What Google saysCommodity content — think "7 Tips for First-Time Homebuyers" — adds little, because anyone (or any AI) could write it. First-hand experience and a genuine point of view are what stand out across the sources it reviews.

What you doTell the story only you can tell. The deal that almost died. The client you saved. The opinion you'd defend in a room full of skeptics. That's the content a model can't generate on its own — so that's the content it cites.

02

Own your Google Business Profile

What Google saysGoogle names the Business Profile directly as a way for local businesses to surface inside AI responses and across Search results.

What you doTreat your profile like a second homepage. Keep it complete, current, and full of real reviews, and optimize it constantly. For any local brand, it's one of the cleanest sources the AI can pull from — and it links straight back to you.

03

Stop over-producing

What Google saysYou don't need to "chunk" content into tiny pieces, write llms.txt files, or spin up a page for every keyword variation. Mass-producing pages to game rankings can get flagged under the scaled-content-abuse spam policy.

What you doMore doesn't win anymore. Take the content you already have and rewrite it to sound like you — sharper, deeper, human. Volume is a tax now. Voice is the moat.
